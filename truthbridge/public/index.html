Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost to you, including shipping. Our ministry is supported by the generosity of people who want God's Word in every Canadian home.

Who can request a Bible?

Anyone with a mailing address in Canada. We ask for one Bible per household so we can reach as many people as possible.

How long will it take to arrive?

Most Bibles arrive within 2–3 weeks. Remote and northern communities can take a little longer.

Will you contact me or add me to a mailing list?

No. We only use your details to send your Bible (and a shipping note if you gave an email). We never sell or share your information.

Can I request a Bible for someone else?

Yes — please enter their name and address, and make sure they'd be happy to receive it.
