Questions

Frequently asked

Can't find your answer? Email us at contact@truthbridgeministries.org.

Is this really free?

Yes — for any mailing address in Canada, including shipping, while monthly capacity and funding allow. Our ministry is supported by the generosity of people who want God's Word in every Canadian home.

How long until my Bible arrives?

Requests are processed within 1–2 weeks.

Can I request more than one?

No. We send one Bible per person so we can reach as many people as possible.

Why is there a monthly limit?

It keeps the ministry funded and sustainable. We currently send up to 20 Bibles each month. When the month is full, requests reopen on the 1st.

Will you contact me or add me to a mailing list?

No. We only use your details to send your Bible (and a shipping note if you gave an email). We never sell or share your information.

Can I request a Bible for someone else?

Yes — please enter their name and address, and make sure they'd be happy to receive it.

Ready for a Bible of your own?

One Bible per person. Requests are processed within 1–2 weeks.
