Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TruthBridge Ministries respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you request a free Bible and how we use it, in keeping with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

Your name, mailing address, preferred Bible language, and — only if you choose to give them — your email, phone number, how you heard about us, and any note or prayer request.

How we use it

Only to mail your Bible, to send a shipping note if you gave an email, and to pray for any request you share. We keep a record of requests so we can send one Bible per household.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don't add you to mailing lists or newsletters. We share your address only with Canada Post (or another carrier) to deliver your Bible.

How we protect it

Requests are stored in an encrypted database hosted by Cloudflare. Only authorized ministry volunteers can view them.

How long we keep it

We keep request records for up to 24 months after your Bible is shipped, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing info@truthbridgeministries.org.
